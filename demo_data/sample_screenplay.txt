SCENE 01 - INT. DOWNTOWN COFFEE SHOP - DAY

Arjun enters a bustling coffee shop and walks toward the counter. The ambient sound of espresso machines fills the room.

ARJUN
Just an iced Americano with oat milk, please.

BARISTA
Name for the cup?

ARJUN
Arjun.

Arjun grabs his drink and also buys a cold bottle of Coca-Cola from the display case. He sits near the window.
A customer wearing a Nike shirt walks past him toward the door.
From a nearby speaker, Arjun is listening to "Midnight City" by M83.

SCENE 02 - EXT. DOWNTOWN STREET - EVENING

Arjun steps outside into the evening twilight. Neon signs flicker to life along the avenue.
He finishes his Coca-Cola and walks past a glowing glass-front Apple Store.
Across the street, a billboard displays an advertisement for Netflix.
